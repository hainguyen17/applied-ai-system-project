# Activity guides

Listening guidance for common situations. Each section is one retrievable chunk.
`energy` is a target range (0 to 1). `arc` is the energy shape across the playlist:
flat (steady), build (rises), peak (warm up, peak, cool down), wind-down (falls).
`tags` are song mood tags worth a bonus. `instrumental: yes` means vocals compete with the activity.

## study
type: activity
synonyms: study, studying, homework, exam, reading, deep work, coding, writing an essay
energy: 0.25-0.45
arc: flat
instrumental: yes
mood: focused
tags: focused, steady
Lyrics compete with reading and writing, so instrumental tracks work best. Keep tempo steady and avoid big jumps.

## workout
type: activity
synonyms: workout, gym, run, running, lifting, cardio, training, spin class, hiit
energy: 0.75-0.97
arc: peak
mood: intense
tags: motivating, driving
Start a little lower to warm up, hold high energy through the main set, then cool down for the last song or two.

## sleep
type: activity
synonyms: sleep, bedtime, fall asleep, nap, insomnia
energy: 0.10-0.30
arc: wind-down
instrumental: yes
mood: peaceful
tags: dreamy, spacey
Energy should only go down. No sudden drums, no vocals.

## party
type: activity
synonyms: party, dance party, pregame, celebration, birthday
energy: 0.70-0.95
arc: build
mood: euphoric
tags: euphoric, bright
Open with recognisable, mid-energy songs and build toward the biggest tracks.

## road trip
type: activity
synonyms: road trip, drive, driving, commute, car ride, highway
energy: 0.55-0.80
arc: flat
mood: uplifting
tags: driving, bright
Steady mid-to-high energy that keeps a driver alert without being frantic.

## rainy day
type: activity

energy: 0.25-0.45
arc: flat
mood: chill
tags: rainy, cozy
Soft, warm, and indoor-sounding. Lofi, jazz, and acoustic folk fit.
